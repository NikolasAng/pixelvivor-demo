Welcome to the Frontier.
You are a Guardian of the Gate. The world is broken, the Void is awakening, and the ancient seals are fading.
Survive the wilds, uncover the hidden secrets, and prepare for the final confrontation.

1. Getting Started (Your First Run)

  1. Choose Your Hero: Each of the 7 heroes has a unique starting weapon, stats, and home biome.
  2. Set Out: Press the "Set Out" button in the Camp to begin a run.
  3. Move: Use WASD or Arrow Keys to move.
  4. Fight: Your weapons attack automatically. You just need to position yourself.
  5. Level Up: Collect XP Gems from defeated enemies. When the XP bar fills, you will be offered a choice of 3 upgrades.
  6. Survive: The longer you survive, the stronger the enemies become. The run ends when you die.

2. The Camp (Your Home Base)

The Camp is your permanent progression hub. Here you can:

  • Character: Choose your hero.
  • Quest Board: View daily contracts, story chains, and bounties.
  • Inventory: Manage your gear, relics, trinkets, and materials.
  • Sets: Equip Legendary Sets.
  • Store: Buy new weapons and tools with Gold.
  • Inn: Hire companions and familiars.
  • Stable: Buy and equip mounts.
  • Trinkets: Equip up to 3 passive trinkets.
  • Bestiary: View all the enemies you have encountered.
  • Lore: Read the stories and secrets you have discovered.
  • Trophies: View your achievements and cosmetics.

Quick Start: Use the "Quick Start" button to instantly begin a run with your last loadout.

3. Combat & Weapons

  • Core Weapons: You can equip up to 4 weapons. Each weapon can be upgraded up to Level 8.
  • Evolutions: If you have a Level 8 weapon and its required passive item, it will evolve into a Legendary Evolution (e.g., Magic Wand L8 + Ring of Power L5 = Arcane Barrage).
  • Upgrade Cards: Every time you level up, you will be offered 3 random upgrade cards. These include new weapons, stat boosts, and powerful Keystones that change how you play.
  • Horde Events: Every 70-105 seconds, a tactical horde will spawn. Watch for the warning banner and prepare to dodge.

4. The World & Biomes

  • 13 Overworld Biomes: Plains, Forest, Magic Forest, Desert, Snowfield, Swamp, Volcano, Badlands, Crystal Wastes, Deadlands, Fungal Wastes, and more.
  • Pocket Dimensions: The world is home to several hidden dimensions:
    • The Abyss: A submerged realm accessed via the Sunken Shrine in the Swamp. Home of the Abyssal Kraken.
    • The Dreamscape: A surreal nightmare accessed by sleeping 3 times in a row. Features a unique Dream Coin economy.
    • The Chicken Dimension: A secret, chaotic realm accessed by trading a Rare Relic with the Mad Poultry Farmer in the Plains.
    • The Mountain Biomes: 5 colossal mountain formations scattered across the world. Each contains a unique centerpiece tree and is guarded by unique mountain mobs.
    • The Underworld: A subterranean realm of caves and materials.
  • Dynamic Weather: Rain, Snow, Storms, and Floods change the environment. Lightning can strike, and floods can raise the water level.

5. World Secrets (The Lore)

The world is full of hidden secrets. The UI will not tell you where they are. You must discover them.

  • The Sunken Temple (Swamp): Wait for Night + Flood. Find the Sunken Shrine.
  • The Frozen Heart Ritual (Snowfield): Wait for a Storm. Have the Frozen Core from the Ice Titan. Find the Stone Circle.
  • The Crystal Cave (Crystal Wastes): Wait for Night. Have 10 Crystal Shards. Find the Crystal Cave entrance.
  • The Mad Alchemist's Secret (Fungal Wastes): Wait for a Blood Moon. Have 3 Poisonous Mushrooms. Find the Abandoned House.
  • The Void Portal (Magic Forest): Have the Philosopher's Stone and 5 Void Essences. Find the Void Altar.

The Chain Reaction: Each secret unlocks the next. You cannot skip ahead.

6. Economy & Materials

  • Gold: The standard currency. Used to buy items in the Camp.
  • Chicken Coins: Earned in the Chicken Dimension. Used to buy XP from the Mad Poultry Farmer.
  • Dream Coins: Earned in the Dreamscape. Used to buy weapons and upgrades from the Dream Merchant.
  • Materials: Dropped by bosses and elites. Used to craft permanent upgrades in the Camp (e.g., Ancient Bark, Frozen Core, Void Essence).
  • Void Essences: Required to open the Void Portal. You need 5. You can get them from the 5 World Secrets and from the Cave Boss.

8. Tips for New Players

  1. Don't stand still: Movement is your best defense.
  2. Collect XP Gems: They are the key to getting stronger.
  3. Try different weapons: Each weapon has a unique playstyle.
  4. Look for the warning banner: A horde is coming. Get ready to dodge.
  5. Explore the world: The best secrets are hidden in plain sight.
  6. Talk to NPCs: They often have clues about the world.
  7. Don't be afraid to die: Every run makes you stronger in the Camp.

9. Testing & Dev Commands (For Playtesting)

If you are helping playtest the game, you can access the Dev Settings in the Pause Menu:

  • Fast-Forward: Skip 5 minutes of time.
  • Teleport to Mountain Biomes: Jump to any of the 5 mountain formations.
  • Teleport to Abyss / Dreamscape / Chicken Dimension: Jump directly to the pocket dimensions.
  • Spawn Mountain Biome Mobs: Instantly spawn the unique mobs for any mountain.
